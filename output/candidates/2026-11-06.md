### 선정 이유

**1. World's Best Waves Threatened By Climate Change New Study Finds**

**2. World Title Race: Who Needs What At Pipeline?**

**3. I Know You've Got A Good Kelly Story, Too…**

**4. Shark Roams Flooded Florida Streets After Historic Storm (Video)**
